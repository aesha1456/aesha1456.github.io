About Me

I recently completed my B.E. in Information Technology at Gujarat Technological University (CGPA 9.68/10). My research interest lies broadly in large language models, particularly how they can be made more reliable, knowledge-grounded, and reasoning-capable. My work on KRUTRIM-RAG, a hybrid vector-and-graph retrieval pipeline built during my internship at ISRO's Space Applications Centre, sparked this interest and gave me hands-on experience with the practical challenges of grounding LLM outputs in external knowledge. I'm currently applying to graduate programs for Spring 2027, and eager to explore this space further — including post-training techniques and knowledge representation for language models.

Projects

KRUTRIM-RAG: Hybrid Graph-Vector Retrieval

Designed a retrieval pipeline combining dense vector search (Qdrant) with knowledge-graph traversal (Neo4j) for precision over cross-referenced aerospace technical documents. Built a proposition-based chunking pipeline with Nomic embeddings, integrated GLiNER for entity-aware retrieval, and deployed the full system on-premise via Ollama to meet air-gapped security constraints.

ISRO / Space Applications Centre

Tool Wear Prediction for CNC Machining

Studied real CNC machining parameters on-site at Elecon Engineering, then trained and compared Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, and Decision Trees — Random Forest generalized best. Built an interactive Streamlit application for real-time tool wear prediction and replacement alerts.

Intel & GTU

SMS Spam & Transaction Classifier
